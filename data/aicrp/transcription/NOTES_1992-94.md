Source: Progress report of the AICRP on Agricultural Acarology, 1992-94 (scan, 158 pp; two identical copies uploaded). Most of the report is experiments. Survey material is in Chapter III.1 (printed pp. 40-56). Typed from the page images; the text layer is not usable. Printed page = PDF page minus about 43 in this section.

## Left out

- Navsari's family table (Valsad, Surat, Bharuch: families, specimen counts, plants) has no species names. Not carried.
- Kalyani's text says jute was severely infested by a yellow mite and that Oligonychus coffeae was frequently collected in tea gardens; neither names both host and mite in a usable way for rows. Not carried as host rows (O. coffeae is in the species list).
- Pigeonpea survey (Table 30): village-level rows were collapsed to the district. Gulbarga fields all read "No mites", and Dharwad's Hubli and Shirahatti taluks were free of mites, so those are absent. The column heading is "mite vectors"; it is taken as Aceria cajani, the vector of sterility mosaic named in the text. Table 31 (Navsari villages, no mites) is not carried.
- Experimental tables (efficacy, varietal reaction, population dynamics) are not survey records.

## Uncertain readings and choices

- E01: the bhindi row (Hoskote Low, Rajankunte Moderate) shows the mite only as one entry for both places; read as Tetranychus macfarlanei. "T. rickeri (predator)" on guava is typed as Typhlodromus rickeri.
- E04: the host "Local" (Cheletogenes ornatus, Phytoseius minutus) is kept as printed. Kalka is printed under Himachal Pradesh in the report's introduction but lies in Haryana; the state follows modern geography as elsewhere. Sujanpur is left unresolved (it exists in both Himachal Pradesh and Punjab). "Karlarpur" in the scan is read as Kartarpur. The genus for rows 29-31 (printed "P.") is taken as Phytoseius after P. minutus. Host and locality lists are printed separately, so they are recorded as host_only and place_only rows.
- E05: the host printed as "Litora" is read as Lasora from Aceria cordiae on the same row. "Eriophyes cerenuus" is the printed spelling of E. cernuus. Remarks that sit between rows are given at host level in the remark field.
- E07: the Sitamarhi mite density is 42.75 in the scan; the text paragraph gives the lowest tree infestation as 65.36% (East Champaran), which matches the table.
- Names without a match in the Paper 1 checklist although the family is covered: Tarsonemus occidentalis and Tarsonemus ananas. Eriophyes moringae is joined to Aculus moringeae by crosswalk (assumed same species, see `config/name_crosswalk.csv`).
