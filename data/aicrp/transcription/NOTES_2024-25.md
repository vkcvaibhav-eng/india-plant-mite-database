# Notes: AICRP Progress Report 2024-25 (XXI Group Meeting), chapter 1 "Diversity of plant-associated mites"

Source: `bb76f513-Progress_Report_2024-25_-_AINP_on_Agril._Acarology.pdf` (307 pp, text layer). Blocks J01-J07, typed from the text layer. Printed page numbers are as in the report.

## Recorded
- J01 Bangalore (Table 1): species and host plants for Karnataka and neighbouring states. The 49 eriophyid species are not named in the table. State is "Karnataka; other states (not split)".
- J02 Coimbatore (Tables 4, 5): host plants and mites for Tamil Nadu. In Table 4 the host is printed once for a group of mites, so each mite in the group is paired with that host. In the last rows the host column sits one row off from the mite column; the pairing used is Raoiella indica with banana and coconut, Polyphagotarsonemus latus with chilli, Aceria guerreronis with coconut, A. cajani with red gram, Cheiracus sulcatus with rice, Cisaberoptus kenyae with mango, Aceria jasmini with jasmine, A. pongamiae with pungam.
- J03 Navsari (Table 8): new host and state records only. Table 6 repeats the 2020-21 table with a few changes and is not entered again.
- J04 Ludhiana (Table 9): predatory mites on plants from surveys in Punjab, Jammu, Haryana, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh and Rajasthan. No place per row, so the state is "(not split)". Asterisks in the printed table (new records) are not kept.
- J05 Mashobra (Table 11): mites on hosts in the 12 districts of Himachal Pradesh.
- J06 Jorhat (Tables 12, 13): four districts of Assam; the table does not tie hosts to districts.
- J07 Thrissur (Tables 14, 15): spider mites and other families on hosts in Kerala; predatory mites on five crops with the prey mites in the note.

## Left out
- Kalyani Table 10 and the Bangalore, Mashobra, Jorhat summary tables: counts only.
- Bangalore Table 2 (repeats Table 1 by crop family) and Table 3 (predator families on crops, no species).
- Navsari Table 7 (natural enemies, repeats earlier tables).
- All experimental and biology chapters, and the molecular tables (ITS2, COI accession numbers).

## Uncertain readings
- Numbered placeholders such as "Euseius sp. 1" to "sp. 4" are recorded as "Euseius sp." so the numbering is lost.
- Names with obvious misprints are kept as printed where the checklist join can still match them; Typhlodromus malicolus is printed as melicolus in the "new records" list.
- Table 9 notes that some records are new to the world (Neoseiulella pentaporus, Euseius planocalyx). Both are recorded as printed; neither is in the Paper 1 checklist.
