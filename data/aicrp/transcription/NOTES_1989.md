# AICRP 1989 (Second Workshop, Hisar, 27-28 December 1989): transcription notes

Source: Progress report for 1988-89 presented at the Second Workshop (scan, 210 pp; the file was uploaded twice under different names, byte-identical). The report is mostly technical programmes and experiments. Typed from the page images; the text layer is not usable. Printed page = PDF page minus about 26 (Kalyani, Coimbatore, Ludhiana), 27 (Bangalore), 20-25 (Navsari).

## Left out

- Varanasi's Table 1.1 (Madhya Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh, 42 hosts) gives families only. The medicinal plant survey (17 plants at Lucknow and Varanasi) names families only. Not carried.
- Navsari Table 2 (mites on insects: Leptus, Podopolipus, Macrochelus and others) has insect hosts. Not carried.
- Hisar's survey paragraph (about 40 plant samples, Hisar district; stored grain from Rohtak) names no mite species.
- Kalyani rows with no mite printed: onion, cabbage, cauliflower, squash, mustard, groundnut (field).
- Ludhiana rows printed "no infestation": Citrus reticulata (Abohar), Robinia pseudoacacia (Dholakuan), Cannabis sativa (Kulu) and one row for Zinnia sp.
- Population tables and experiments (Navsari coconut, mango, pigeonpea, sorghum and cotton dynamics, Coimbatore and Kalyani trials) are not carried. The survey rows for pigeonpea, sorghum and cotton at Surat and Bharuch record only that the named mite was present.

## Uncertain readings and choices

- F01: the survey year printed for cotton is "Nov. 1989" in a report on 1988-89; kept as printed. Hosts are common names as printed. Names normalised: Polyphagotersonemus to Polyphagotarsonemus, Tryophagus putricentiae to Tyrophagus putrescentiae (not in the checklist families), Toberne-montana to Tabernaemontana; the printed forms are in the remarks. Mushidabad is read as Murshidabad. Mahimada (Muzaffarpur, Bihar), Puri, Udala and Chandikhal (Odisha) are given those states. Places and mites for rose, hollyhock and the dahlia row use ditto marks and are read from the row above.
- F02: the printed "Cisaberoptes", "Cheiracus" and "javrimovici" are typed as Cisaberoptus, Cheiracus and jawrimovici (Calacarus jawrimovici). Anthocoptes "n.sp." is typed as sp. nov. "Tetra sp." on Pithacolobium dulse is an eriophyoid genus as printed. The report covers Tamil Nadu and Kerala without saying which record comes from which state, so the state is "Tamil Nadu; Kerala (not split)" and these rows are left out of the state comparison.
- F03: the right edge of the scan is clipped; completed names are Brevipalpus obovatus, Oligonychus mangiferae and Terminalichus (printed Terminalicus). Rows for Amaranthus and plum have no place or date in the scan. Dholakuan is in Himachal Pradesh (Sirmaur).
- F04: Hyderabad is recorded under Telangana, its state today. Kalyani Dam (Thirupathi) is Andhra Pradesh. "Crallia bracteata" is typed as printed. The rows with only "Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh" as the place have no place, so their state is "Karnataka; Andhra Pradesh (not split)".
- F06: "Q." in the sorghum survey text stands for the sorghum mite; Oligonychus indicus is read from the figure label. The cotton mite is printed T. (telarius) cinnabarinus.
- Names without a match in the checklist although the family is covered: Aceria punicae, Brevipalpus punicae, Calacarus jawrimovici, Dolichotetranychus palmivora, Eotetranychus strychnosi, Obuloides rajmohani, Anthocoptes bambusovagrans, Rhombacus morrisi. Eriophyes mangiferae, Eriophyes ailanthes (assumed Aceria ailanthae), Amblyseius herbiculous, A. ovallis and Petrobia hartii are joined by crosswalk.
