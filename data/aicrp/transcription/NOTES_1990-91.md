## Not transcribed

- Bangalore (pp. 20-21): the survey section lists only localities (Raichur district, Shimoga district, Kolar, Bangalore district, Visakhapatnam and Vizianagaram districts in Andhra Pradesh). Species appear in Chapter II (intensive taxonomic study).
- Hisar Table 7 (plants surveyed) and the Pusa districts Purnea, Katihar, Bhojpur, Rohtas, Jehanabad, Gaya and Nalanda (slides not yet identified at the time of the report).
- Coimbatore rows 14 (Thondamuthur, jack) and 15 (Somathur, anona): the mite column has no family or name.
- Varanasi: hosts with no mite found (Datura metel, Commiphora wightii, Eriobotrya japonica, Cucumis melo, Pisum sativum, Phoenix rupicola, wheat) and "Barhar" (family not legible).
- Chapter II (printed pp. 40-55), the taxonomic study centre by centre.

## Uncertain readings (flagged in the `remark` column)

- Kalyani 15 (litchi) and Varanasi 13 (castor): the Amblyseius (Typhlodromips) name is partly illegible; kept at genus level.
- Kalyani 19 (lemon): Brevipalpus and Amblyseius (Amblyseius) names illegible; genus level.
- Kalyani 34 (Ziziphus): printed as "Eutetranychus maximae? Nassar & Ghai"; kept at genus level.
- Coimbatore: the unnumbered "Coconut / Phytoseiidae" line after row 40 and the "Amblyseius ovalis" line after row 45 are assigned by position.
- Varanasi 39 (Sishum): "Tetra. sisso" read as Tetranychus sp.
- Ludhiana: specimen counts for T. cinnabarinus and B. phoenicis are not legible; counts for other rows are in the `remark` column.
- Pusa 'Amblyseius homalii Gupta' is printed with the genus Amblyseius; it is joined to Typhlodromus homalii by the crosswalk.
- Navsari host "Tirdex sp." and "Ekalifa" are as printed.
- Varanasi: sampling sites are not transcribed for hosts where the site column could not be read reliably; those rows carry the centre state only.
