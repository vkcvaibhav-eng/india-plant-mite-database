# Notes: AICRP Progress Report 2021-22 (XVIII Group Meeting), survey chapter III.1

Source: `51065290-Progress_Report_2021-22_-_AINP.pdf` (245 pp, text layer). Blocks I01-I04.

## Recorded
- I01 Coimbatore (Table 127): place, host, mite for Tamil Nadu, plus the ITS2-confirmed identifications.
- I02 Ludhiana (Table 130): the 2021-22 version of the Punjab table, now with Rajasthan localities (Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Durgapura, Jaipur) and Sirsa in Haryana. Hosts and localities are separate lists. GPS coordinates omitted.
- I03 Jorhat (Tables 136, 137): only the rows that are new compared with 2020-21 (a Dillenia record, Brevipalpus pulchur and B. chilensis, Amblyseius alstoniae, Rhizoglyphus). The remaining rows repeat the 2020-21 table.
- I04 Thrissur (Table 138): 16 spider mite species on 35 hosts in Kerala; coordinates omitted.

## Left out
- Navsari Table 128 repeats Table 115 of the 2020-21 report almost word for word (one added host: betelvine for Brevipalpus phoenicis). Not entered again.
- Bangalore (Tables 125-126): counts only.
- Kalyani: only narrative for Darjeeling, North Dinajpur and South Dinajpur districts, with no species by host table.
- Experimental chapters and the natural enemy chapter III.2.

## Uncertain readings
- Coimbatore host and mite codes such as "T. urticae" were expanded to full names; "P. latus" is Polyphagotarsonemus latus.
- Blackgram at Bhavanisagar is placed in Erode district as printed.

## Mashobra status tables (added 11 November 2026)
Block I05, Tables 132-135 (pdf 167-176, printed 158-167), read from the text layer and checked against page images where cells wrap. The survey ran April 2021 to March 2022 across Shimla, Kinnaur, Kullu, Mandi, Solan, Hamirpur, Bilaspur and Kangra districts; Table 132's caption says 2020-21, which is taken as a misprint.
- ERM is entered as Panonychus ulmi and TSM as Tetranychus urticae; the text names both ("European red mite, P. ulmi and Two-spotted mite, T. urticae").
- "Flat red mite" and "flat mite" are entered as Tenuipalpidae. The pear rust mite reported in the text from Theog, Jubbal and Rohru is entered as Eriophyidae.
- Not entered: "yellow mite", "black mite", "red mite" (mango) and "unidentified" entries, which cannot be tied to a taxon; predator counts (predators are not named); the mushroom mite survey.
- Each row is one host at one place; months are in the note, so a host seen in two months is one row.
- Generic place names (Rampur, Bilaspur, Nagar, Neri, Indora, Deha, Kiari, Sakri) are not added to the place table and take the block state, Himachal Pradesh.
- Ramtori (a gourd) and Mushki Kapoor are local names left unresolved in the host columns.
