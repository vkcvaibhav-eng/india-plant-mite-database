# Check of the transcriptions against the reports (12-13 November 2026)

## Reports with a text layer

`scripts/tools/verify_against_pdf.py` looks for the mite epithet, the host and the place of each observation row on the same or the next PDF page.

| Report | Rows | Found together on first pass |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1996-98 | 1,109 | 1,038 |
| 2020-21 | 1,978 | 1,910 |
| 2021-22 | 317 | 225 |
| 2024-25 | 757 | 746 |

Every row that was not found was read against the PDF text. None was a wrong record. The causes were:

- a printed spelling that was normalised when typed (Zizyphus to Ziziphus, Bauhenia to Bauhinia, "Peanut pintoi (ornamental)" to "Arachis pintoi (Peanut)", Oregan, Cyclaman, Candula);
- a table row that continues over a page break, so mite and host sit two pages apart;
- the Mashobra tables, which print ERM, TSM and "flat mite" where the transcription has *Panonychus ulmi*, *Tetranychus urticae* and Tenuipalpidae. Run with `--alias`, 241 of the 247 Mashobra rows are found; the other six are the spelling corrections above.

## Scanned reports (1988, 1989, 1990, 1990-91, 1992-94 and the scanned part of 1996-98)

Twelve pages were drawn at random and each transcription line from them (about 150 lines) was compared with the page image. All matched. One family-level row (1990-91, Varanasi, *Tinospora cordifolia*, Tenuipalpidae) lacked its place; Varanasi was added.

## What this check does not cover

It confirms that what is typed is what is printed. It does not test whether the centre's identification was right, and for the scanned reports it is a sample, not a full second reading.
