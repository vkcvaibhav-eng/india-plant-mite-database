## Not transcribed

- Kalyani (p. 38-39): narrative only. Survey of 36 crops in ten districts, no per-species list. Species named in the text (Dolichotetranychus floridanus, Polyphagotarsonemus latus, Oligonychus coffeae, Eriophyes mangiferae, Brevipalpus phoenicis, Aceria litchi, Raoiella indica, Tetranychus fijiensis, Eutetranychus orientalis) are not tied to hosts and places.
- Navsari (p. 68): abstract only (250 samples, six districts of Maharashtra and four of Gujarat, 224 slides sent to Bangalore).
- Bangalore (pp. 23, 25): counts of species by family for Dharwad, North Kanara, Bangalore and Chittoor. No names.
- Coimbatore section 2.2.1 (eriophyid list with new species, pp. 31-33): partly repeats the survey list; new species named only there (for example Neodicrothrix piperae, Paraphytoptus jujubae, Eriophyes ensifolia) are not transcribed.
- Ludhiana Table II rows without a legible mite name; slide numbers of Hisar material.
- Varanasi Table 1.4 rows 10, 11 and 20 (unidentified mites).
- Varanasi: the '*' that marks a new host record for a mite is not carried per host.
- Hisar: report not received.

## Uncertain readings

- Bangalore: 'Rane Bennur, Bangalore' is one locality entry for coconut, and 'Manchenahalli, Mysore' is read as one locality.
- Coimbatore: host names cut at the page edge (Euochloa reptans, Jasminum sambac, Murraya koenigii, Rosa sinensis) are completed from context. Eriophyoid authority 'MMS' is as printed.
- Ludhiana Table I: pairing of each host with its localities is read from the vertical alignment. 'B. kanachiensis' is printed that way (the text prints 'krachiensis'); it is joined to Brevipalpus karachiensis through the crosswalk. Brevipalpus 'rica' (Table II) is as printed.
- Varanasi Table 1.2: row 17 (Agistemus industani) has a third host line overprinted at the page break; row 23's species name is overprinted and recorded as Phytoseius (Pennaseius) sp. Row 9 (Tetranychus urticae) is printed under a Brevipalpus family heading that belongs to row 10.
- Varanasi Table 1.1 spelling 'homalli', 'putriscantiae', 'Berless' are kept in the printed form in the source text and corrected in the columns where they would not join.
- Varanasi Table 1.4: column alignment of the + signs was read from rotated scans; weed names follow the table heads.
